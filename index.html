Market Day in My Village

October 03, 2026 by Jayden Thompson

This week I finally finished the village market I have been planning for a month. It started as a handful of wooden stalls around the well, but it has grown into a full street with colourful umbrellas, palm trees and a stall for every kind of trade. I traded with the villagers for emeralds all week, and the farmer's stall now sells the best carrots in the entire world.

The part I am proudest of is the bike path that runs through the middle of the market. It is not a real vehicle, of course, but with a few custom textures and some clever use of minecarts it feels like one. Riding it at sunrise while the villagers set up their stalls is my new favourite way to start a play session. Next, I want to add a night market with lanterns and glow berries so the whole street lights up after dark.

Exploring the Cherry Grove

October 10, 2026 by Jayden Thompson

On Saturday I set out to explore a cherry grove I spotted from the top of a mountain. The pink trees and falling petals make it one of the prettiest biomes in the game, and I could not resist spending the whole afternoon wandering around with my magnifying glass, looking for anything unusual. I found a family of rabbits, a small cave full of copper ore and a perfect hilltop to build a cabin on.

Exploring always teaches me something about planning ahead, which is a lot like what we do in our programming classes. Before leaving I packed food, torches, a pickaxe and a spare bed, so I was never stuck far from home. Whenever I plan a project, I try to do the same thing: gather my tools first, take it one step at a time and always keep a backup. The cabin build will be the topic of my next post.
